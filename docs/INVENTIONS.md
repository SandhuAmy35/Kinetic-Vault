# Inventions

- Local expiry gate refuses new Reveal unlocks before and after decryption when the library expiry has passed, with recovery by changing or clearing expiry in Vault; it uses the device clock and local metadata, never erases files, and cannot expire exported copies or an already open secret.
- Private gesture replay animates matched enrollment moves on demand in a screenshot-protected dialog, names every move for accessibility, respects disabled system animations, and wipes its transient token copy on completion, dismissal, or pause.
- Comparison proof sheet shares a PNG file containing LSB-stripped before/after previews, a coarse 16×16 change-density map and measured source statistics after a cover-disclosure warning, without plaintext or credentials and without claiming cryptographic proof.
